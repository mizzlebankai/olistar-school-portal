Educational Philosophy

Laying the Groundwork for Life-Long Mastery

In alignment with our founding motto, "Good Foundation, Firm Building," the Primary Division at Olistar School provides an inspiring, safe, and academically rigorous environment. We ensure every child develops a confident grasp of core concepts before transitioning to higher academic tiers.

"A child whose primary foundation is firm stands unshakable through senior academic and technical pursuits."

Academic Progression

Standards-Based Curriculum Structure

Following Ghana Education Service (GES) national standards enhanced with enriched institutional modules.

Classes 1 – 3

Lower Primary Continuum

Focuses on active learning, language development, basic scientific observation, and fundamental mathematical operations through engaging classroom activities.

Core Subject Areas:
  • English Language: Listening, Speaking, Reading & Writing
  • Mathematics: Numbers, Measurement & Geometry
  • Our World Our People (OWOP): Social & Environmental Awareness
  • Religious and Moral Education (RME): Values & Discipline
  • Creative Arts: Drawing, Craftwork & Performing Arts
  • Ghanaian Language: Local Language & Cultural Heritage
Classes 4 – 6

Upper Primary Continuum

Prepares pupils for abstract critical thinking, practical science experimentation, computing skills, and structured examination readiness before JHS transition.

Core Subject Areas:
  • English Language & Grammar: Literature & Advanced Composition
  • Mathematics & Problem Solving: Fractions, Algebra Prep & Data
  • Integrated Science: Biological & Physical Science Inquiry
  • Computing / ICT: Computer Systems, Software & Internet Literacy
  • Our World Our People (OWOP): Geography, Citizenship & Civics
  • Religious and Moral Education (RME) & Creative Arts
Campus Life

Conducive Learning Environment

We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized teacher-pupil interaction, allowing educators to identify learning strengths and support individual development areas early.

Dedicated ICT & Computing Access

Primary pupils receive weekly hands-on laboratory lessons guided by specialized ICT tutors.

Sports & Co-Curricular Clubs

Weekly physical education, debate, readers' club, and cultural troupe activities.
