JHS Core & Elective Subjects

BECE Core Foundations

Core Academic Disciplines

Intensive instruction focused on high performance in WAEC Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) core papers.

Core Subjects:
  • Core Mathematics: Algebra, Geometry, Statistics & Word Problems
  • English Language: Grammar, Essay Composition & Comprehension
  • Integrated Science: Biology, Physics, Chemistry & Lab Experiments
  • Social Studies: Governance, Geography, Environment & History
Digital & Applied Knowledge

Computing & Career Technology

Equips learners with practical IT expertise, internet navigation, basic algorithms, technical drawing principles, and workshop materials.

Subject Highlights:
  • Computing (ICT): Software Applications, Systems & Internet Safety
  • Career Technology: Materials, Technical Tools & Design Basics
  • Creative Arts & Design: Visual Art, Graphics & Spatial Drafting
  • Hands-on Computer Lab sessions every week
Communication Skills

Ghanaian Language & French

Developing multilingual competency in Ghanaian heritage languages (Twi) alongside introductory French to expand global communication skills.

Subject Highlights:
  • Ghanaian Language (Twi): Literature, Culture & Grammar
  • French Language: Basic Conversation, Grammar & Vocabulary
  • Cultural heritage appreciation and debate clubs
Ethics & Discipline

Religious & Moral Education (RME)

Reinforcing moral discipline, civic duty, ethics, and respect for community values to prepare mature youth for senior secondary transition.

Subject Highlights:
  • Moral decision-making and character building
  • Religious studies and civic responsibility
  • Prefectural leadership and student council participation
Pathway to the Future

Direct Pathway to Olistar SHS & Technical Streams

Olistar JHS graduates enjoy priority admission into our specialized Senior High, ICT, Building Construction, Home Economics, and General Arts programs right on the same campus.

Technical Orientation Early On

JHS students interact with technical drawing tools, ICT labs, and workshop tutors before choosing their senior high stream.

Mock Examination Series

Regular BECE standard mock exams ensure pupils are thoroughly familiar with WAEC question formats and time management.

Enroll in Olistar JHS Today

Placements available for JHS 1, JHS 2, and BECE transfer students.
