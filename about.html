Our Guiding Philosophy

"Good Foundation, Firm Building"

At Olistar Educational Complex, we believe that academic excellence must be built upon an unshakeable foundation of moral discipline, critical thought, and practical capability.

From our primary classrooms to our senior technical workshops, our institutional purpose remains unchanged: to raise well-rounded leaders equipped with both formal academic rigor and hands-on competence required to solve real-world challenges in Ghana and beyond.

Academic Rigor

Comprehensive curriculum following GES and NABPTEX/CTVET standards.

Practical Skills

Direct hands-on technical training integrated with general studies.

Core Pillars of Ethos

Integrity & Character

Cultivating moral discipline, accountability, and respect within a supportive school environment.

Technical Skill & Innovation

Bridging academic theory with practical application across ICT, engineering basics, and craft trades.

Community & Leadership

Instilling civic responsibility and collaborative leadership qualities in every student.

Our Journey

Institutional History

Key milestones, based on the founding proprietor's recollections. Some dates and details are still being confirmed.

1987
Founding Foundation

Olistar Educational Complex began on August 16 in a modest clay building in Abesim. Starting small, the institution has steadily grown over the years to serve a vibrant community of learners.

1992
JHS & Technical Milestones

In 1992, the school's inaugural JHS cohort sat for their exams, achieving a 100% pass rate with all ten students succeeding. That same year, the school received its technical institute certificate, introducing foundational courses in Building and Construction and Electrical Installation.

Site B
A Growing School

To accommodate rapid expansion and a thriving population of around 800 students, Olistar Educational Complex established its Site B campus, marking a major milestone in its ongoing development.
