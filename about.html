Our Guiding Philosophy

"Good Foundation, Firm Building"

At Olistar School, we believe that academic excellence must be built upon an unshakeable foundation of moral discipline, critical thought, and practical capability.

From our primary classrooms to our senior technical workshops, our institutional purpose remains unchanged: to raise well-rounded leaders equipped with both formal academic rigor and hands-on competence required to solve real-world challenges in Ghana and beyond.

Academic Rigor

Comprehensive curriculum following GES and NABPTEX/CTVET standards.

Practical Skills

Direct hands-on technical training integrated with general studies.

Core Pillars of Ethos

Integrity & Character

Cultivating moral discipline, accountability, and respect within a supportive school environment.

Technical Skill & Innovation

Bridging academic theory with practical application across ICT, engineering basics, and craft trades.

Community & Leadership

Instilling civic responsibility and collaborative leadership qualities in every student.

Our Journey

Institutional History

Key milestones, based on the founding proprietor's recollections. Some dates and details are still being confirmed.

1987
Founding Foundation

Olistar School began on 16 August in a modest clay building in Abesim. It started small and grew over time to serve more learners.

1992
JHS & Technical Milestones

The proprietor recalls that the first JHS cohort sat its exams in 1992 and all ten students passed. That year, the school also received its technical institute certificate, with Building and Construction and Electrical Installation among its early courses.

Site B
A Growing School

The school later opened Site B. The proprietor recalls that enrollment was about 800 students at that time; the opening date is still being confirmed.

Symbolism of the Crest

Open Book

Represents academic enlightenment, continuous inquiry, and mastery of the written word.

Gear & Anvil

Symbolizes practical technical skills, industrial competence, and hands-on craftsmanship.

Golden Torch

Denotes moral clarity, leadership guidance, and the light of truth passing through generations.
