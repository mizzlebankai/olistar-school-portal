The Dual Mandate

Scholarship & Craft

At Olistar Educational Complex, we believe intellectual development and manual proficiency are not mutually exclusive. Our upper division is uniquely structured into two parallel, highly respected pathways tailored to a student's natural aptitude and career aspirations.

Begin Enrollment

SHS Academic Stream

WASSCE Syllabus

Intensive preparation across General Arts and Business tracks, designed exclusively for students pursuing university degrees in humanities, law, commerce, and social sciences.

Technical / TVET Stream

CTVET & NVTI Cert.

Hands-on, workshop-based tracks focusing on employable industrial skills, software development, building construction, and hospitality.

Vocational Excellence

Technical Core Tracks (TVET)

ICT & Software

From fundamental computer literacy to advanced networking and web development. Students learn to build, troubleshoot, and maintain modern hardware and software ecosystems.

  • Web Development Basics
  • PC Hardware & Networking
  • Office Productivity Suites

Building Construction

Grounded in structural integrity and architectural drafting. This track combines physical masonry and carpentry with Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software principles.

  • Technical Drawing & CAD
  • Masonry & Site Practices
  • Construction Materials Science

Catering & Home Econ.

Focused on hospitality management, culinary arts, and food science. Prepares students for immediate deployment in the hospitality sector or advanced tertiary food science programs.

  • Culinary Arts & Baking
  • Hospitality Management
  • Nutrition & Food Science
