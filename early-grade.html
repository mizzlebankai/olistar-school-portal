Foundational Pathways

Our Early Childhood Divisions

Tailored learning stages designed for crucial early developmental milestones.

Creche & Toddlers

Ages 1 – 2 Years

Safe, loving care focusing on social integration, sensory development, basic language skills, and interactive play in a secure environment.

Nursery 1 & 2

Ages 3 – 4 Years

Introducing structured play, letter recognition, Jolly Phonics, foundational numbers, fine motor development, and creative arts.

Kindergarten (KG 1 & 2)

Ages 4 – 6 Years

Preparing young learners for Primary education with early reading, numeracy, social skills, problem solving, and science exploration.

Child-Centered Excellence

Why Parents Choose Olistar Early Grade

Safe & Child-Friendly Facilities

Supervised, air-cooled classrooms and fenced play areas built specifically for young safety and comfort.

Caring & Trained Caregivers

Experienced early childhood educators trained in early childhood psychology, patience, and activity-based learning.

Play-Based & Phonics Curriculum

GES-aligned early childhood curriculum enhanced with Jolly Phonics, rhymes, storytelling, and tactile learning kits.

Holistic Social & Moral Growth

Instilling good manners, sharing, self-care routines, and mutual respect right from the earliest years.

Core Early Curriculum Pillars

Literacy & Phonics

Letter sounds, vocabulary acquisition, speech development, and early reading fluency.

Numeracy & Logic

Counting, shape recognition, pattern matching, and spatial understanding.

Creative Arts

Drawing, coloring, nursery rhymes, music, and motor skill building exercises.

Our World & Science

Exploring nature, body awareness, health habits, and environmental curiosity.
