Founding Vision & Legacy

"A Firm Building Requires an Unshakeable Foundation"

In 1987, Olistar School was established in Sunyani Abesim with a singular, transformative vision: to provide children in the Bono region and beyond with access to disciplined, high-caliber basic education coupled with practical technical skills.

Driven by a deep commitment to community empowerment, our Founder recognized that academic literacy alone was incomplete without moral fortitude and technical capability. What began as a modest community primary school has expanded into a comprehensive educational institution comprising Primary, Junior High (JHS), and Senior Technical streams.

"When you equip a young person with both character and practical hands-on mastery, you give them the key to build their own future and strengthen their society."

— Oliver Star-K. Baah, Founder
